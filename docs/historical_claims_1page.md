# Claim summary — revised evidence, 19 October 2026

**Scope.** A concrete GPU software implementation and a conditional memory–completion result for exact global gap-affine full traceback. Match=0, mismatch=2, gap(L)=3+L; no band or heuristic. One RTX 4060 Ti/WSL host, finite configurations, public paired benchmarks. Automatic selection and new hardware are future work.

**Established in this artifact.** The corrected common kernel removes three inherited shared-writer sites. A block-local mark/rank/staged-compact/remap collector reuses a private traceback arena without a second full relocation arena. The root argument is tied to the fixed recurrence and five rolling slots; independent DP, actual-helper tests and full public CIGAR validation supply finite complementary checks.

On all 10,000 public ONT 50k-file pairs, the same binary at C20M/w32/E16384 reserves exactly 5,474.008 MiB in both modes. Five fresh-process, crossed, detailed-instrumentation-off runs per mode give medians 28.121 s OFF and 24.320 s Full, a 13.5% reduction. Supported-pair CPU rescues fall from 118 to zero; 888 unsupported-length pairs remain CPU-routed. The effect includes continued GPU work, avoided CPU recomputation, overlap and output completion. It is not pure collector speedup.

**Conditional findings.** Full also beats the fastest tested lighter guarded setting under each 3/5.5 GiB reservation cap by 17.5%/11.8%. This is a finite operating-point comparison, not equal-byte allocation between every cross-binary pair. Full10/20 each generate 19,939,875,345 records, while retention falls at collections. The posthoc witness 50k:02647 generates 39.264M records in a 10M arena and retains at most 4.799M at its eight collections. That is neither a continuously measured live peak nor an algorithmic lower bound.

**Negative and unestablished findings.** CPU BiWFA20 is competitive on ONT50k and faster on refreshed short-ONT and CCS. Short GPU ranges overlap. Smaller E8192 increases exact CPU rescue and loses the tested full-job comparison. The old 12.7% result is preserved but not pooled: inherited same-value shared writes prevent retrospective clearance of its timing. Its last STOPPED diagnostic is excluded. Sanitizer initialization is unavailable under WDDM; no race-free certification follows from exit code zero. Physical VRAM residency/OOM, arbitrary penalties, prefix qualification, all exact implementations, independent donors/arrival traces, a general selector and hardware necessity remain unproved.

**Paper decision.** A six-page ICEIC implementation/mechanism study is supported, with GC attribution to WFA2 and explicit baseline boundaries. General execution principles are suggestive; priority of a broader new technique is not established. Next: freeze evaluation on one provenance-clear independent ONT read–reference corpus before selector development.
